Laravel EdTech School Management Result Checker

EduMetric EIMS (School Management System)

The Industry Gap: Primary and secondary schools spend 3+ weeks at the end of every term calculating continuous assessment (CA) and exam marks by hand, causing arithmetic mistakes, delayed report cards, and tense parent-teacher confrontations.

How I Came In: Developed a single-tenant Educational Institution Management System (EIMS) with continuous assessment grading (20/20/60), single-click terminal report card generation with positions and remarks, and a secure parent online result checker.

Significance & Outcome: Slashed report card compilation from 3 weeks to 10 minutes, completely eliminated manual calculation errors, and provided parents with instant digital result access.
