02 · Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before we begin.

What is your typical project timeline?

Standard marketing websites and landing pages ship within two weeks. Custom Laravel web applications and multi-vendor systems generally take three to six weeks depending on database complexity and custom integrations.

How are payments structured?

Engagements typically run on a 50% deposit before kickoff and 50% upon final sign-off prior to production deployment. For long-term development retainers or monthly care plans, invoicing occurs monthly.

Can you work with existing teams or codebases?

Yes, I regularly collaborate with remote development teams and integrate with existing Git repositories. I conduct an initial codebase and schema audit before committing new architectural features.

Do you write the copy or just the code?

I handle both technical engineering and high-converting copy so your messaging matches your system architecture. Having both planned together guarantees cohesive user journeys and higher conversion rates.
