02 · Biography

How analytical logic meets digital architecture.

I am a full-stack web developer and a graduate of Physics from the University of Jos. I have been building websites for clients since 2017, starting with custom HTML and PHP, moving into Laravel backends, WordPress client systems, and modern React interfaces.

Studying physics changed how I write software. Physics trains you to eliminate assumptions, inspect the boundary conditions, and build mathematical models that match reality. On the web, that means normalized database design, zero unnecessary JavaScript bloat, clean error handling, and performance that holds up under real network constraints.

I also craft the copy as well as the code. Having words and system architecture planned together means clients get cohesive digital platforms that communicate value clearly and convert visitors without ambiguity.

Based in Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria, I work with local business owners and ship for clients worldwide. I direct Life Untold Enterprises, serve as Editor-in-Chief for the National Association of Physics Students (NAPS), and actively participate in Google Developer Group (GDG Jos) and Microsoft Developers Group.
