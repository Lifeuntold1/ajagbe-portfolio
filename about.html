02 — Biography

How the code and copy connect.

I study Physics at the University of Jos. I have also been building websites for clients since 2017. The two fit together better than you would expect.

Physics gave me a habit. When something breaks, I measure before I guess. A slow page has a cause. A page that does not convert has a cause. I find the cause and fix that one thing.

I write the copy as well as the code, so the words and the layout are planned together. You deal with one person from the first call to launch day.

I live in Jos. That means I can sit with a business owner in person, and I can also ship for clients in other countries. I run Life Untold Enterprises, edit for the National Association of Physics Students Nigeria, and take part in the Google Developer Group and Microsoft Developers Group in Jos.
